Privacy

Simple by default.

Ask Shropshire is designed to collect as little personal information as practical and keep private submissions out of the public data feed.

Location

Your browser is asked for location only when you tap “Use my location”. It is used in the browser to help rank distance, weather and routes. If you do not grant permission, you can choose an area manually. Precise location is not included in alert subscriptions or search-feedback records.

Saved items and shared plans

Saved places/events and locally recorded weak-search history are stored in your browser. Shared day-plan links contain the selected date, area and chosen public listing IDs/names so another person can open the plan. Do not put private information in a shared plan.

Public forms

Event submissions, business claims, business-listing updates, alert signups, offer submissions, missing-result feedback and privacy requests use Jotform. The form provider processes what you submit so Ask Shropshire can review the request. Only ask for information needed for that purpose and do not submit passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information.

Private admin storage

Moderation records, business claims, alert subscriptions, search analytics and other private admin data are stored separately from the public feed in Airtable. Approved public facts may later be copied into the public event/place/offer feeds, but private contact details, claims, alert emails and admin notes are not published.

Email alerts

If you opt in to alerts, Ask Shropshire stores your email address, chosen area/radius, categories and frequency so relevant alerts can be sent through the connected email service. Alerts are optional. You can stop them by replying UNSUBSCRIBE to an Ask Shropshire alert or by making a privacy request.

Search feedback

When you report a missing result, the search query, town/area and category/context may be copied into private Search Analytics so coverage gaps can be reviewed. An optional reply email is not copied into the analytics table.

Maps, weather and routing

The beta may make direct browser requests to Open-Meteo for weather, the public OSRM routing service for route estimates, OpenStreetMap tiles for maps, and Google Maps when you choose a directions/transit link. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Credentials and security

Airtable credentials and private admin access are not embedded in the public app. Public users submit information through the approved form routes rather than writing directly to the private database.

Your privacy choices

You can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held for Ask Shropshire, withdraw alert consent, object to certain uses, or ask a privacy question. Identity may need to be verified before personal information is disclosed or deleted.

Privacy / data request

Retention and launch details

Records are kept only while they are needed for moderation, verification, alerts, service operation, security or legal requirements. The final named data-controller contact and detailed retention schedule must be added before the public production launch; this beta notice will be updated when those details are finalised.
