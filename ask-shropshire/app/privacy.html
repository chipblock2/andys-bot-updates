Privacy
Privacy

Simple by default.

Ask Shropshire is designed to use as little personal information as possible.

Location

Your browser is asked for location only when you tap “Use my location”. The app uses it to rank distance, weather and routes. If you do not grant permission, you can choose an area manually.

Saved items

Saved places and events are currently stored in your browser using local storage. They are not uploaded to the Ask Shropshire admin database.

External services

The beta may make direct browser requests to Open-Meteo for weather, the public OSRM routing service for route estimates, OpenStreetMap tiles for maps, and Google Maps only when you choose a directions or transit link. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Public content

The public app reads approved event, place, offer and local-tip data from a read-only public feed. Private business claims, alert subscriptions, admin notes, analytics records and contact details are not included in that public feed.

Credentials

Airtable credentials and private admin access are not embedded in the public app.

Future accounts and alerts

If email alerts, business claims or account features are enabled later, Ask Shropshire will show the purpose, consent and retention information before collecting personal data.
