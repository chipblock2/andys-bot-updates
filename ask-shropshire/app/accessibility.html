Accessibility
Accessibility

Useful without making you fight the interface.

Ask Shropshire aims to work well with keyboard navigation, screen readers, zoom and reduced-motion settings.

Keyboard and focus

Main controls have visible focus states and the app includes a skip-to-content link. Buttons and links are designed with mobile-sized touch targets.

Screen-reader support

Form controls are labelled, live status areas announce updates, decorative icons are hidden from assistive technology, and the map has a text alternative through the results list.

Motion

If your device requests reduced motion, transitions and smooth scrolling are disabled.

Map alternatives

You do not need to use the map to get results. The same places and events remain available in the list, with directions and travel links.

Venue accessibility data

Ask Shropshire distinguishes verified access information from unknown information. Missing details are not treated as accessible by default. For important access needs, confirm directly with the venue before travelling.

Current beta status

This is still a beta. A final manual screen-reader, keyboard-only and zoom test remains part of the pre-launch test pass.
